# Editorial checks

Use these as editing questions, not a phrase blacklist or an AI detector.

## Meaning before polish

Can a reader tell what happened, why the author cared, and what the result means?
Keep details that answer those questions. Cut repeated claims and ornamental
introductions, not the explanation itself. A short technical limitation often
earns more trust than another benefit. Do not replace a missing fact with a
plausible statistic or remove uncertainty just to make the sentence smoother.

## Voice

Prefer ordinary verbs and specific nouns. In Russian, reconsider phrases such as
"в рамках", "осуществлять", "уникальный", "раскрывает потенциал" and "важно отметить"
when they add no meaning. They are not forbidden in a precise quotation or a
context where they carry real information.

Avoid a succession of slogans, identical paragraph lengths, forced groups of
three, repeated "not X but Y" contrasts and grand conclusions. Natural rhythm
comes from explaining different ideas, not random sentence-length variation.
Keep intentional repetition when it is clearer than inventing synonyms.

Do not insert unsupported opinions or anecdotes. Do not break a logical account
with artificial chaos, unfinished thoughts or pretend frustration. When the user
says "simpler", reduce friction while preserving the content and their expertise.

Use normal keyboard punctuation in newly written prose. Do not silently rewrite
exact quotes, code, links or notation. Avoid heavy bolding, emoji headings and
decorative separators unless the user requests them for this format.

## Synthetic examples, not user history

Personal project announcement, facts supplied: a developer built a local report
tool, it reads CSV files, and large files are still slow.

Too promotional:
"Meet a revolutionary tool that unlocks your productivity. Try it today!"

Too thin:
"Made a thing. It reads files. Here's the code."

Useful middle:
"I made a small tool for the reports I kept assembling by hand. It reads CSV
files and puts the totals in one place. Large files are still slow, so that's
what I'm working on next."

Russian equivalent:
"Сделал небольшой инструмент для отчётов, которые раньше собирал вручную.
Он читает CSV и сводит итоги в одном месте. На больших файлах пока тормозит,
с этим ещё разбираюсь."

Do not reuse this anecdote unless it is true for the current author. A user can
prefer "сделал" in personal announcements and still use "выпустили" in company
release notes. Save the context with the preference.

## Practice sources and limits

This is an original, compact workflow informed by the installed `humanizer-ru`,
`natural-writing` and `write-like-me` practices: cut inflated claims and filler,
preserve meaning, and use authorized samples. It does not copy their examples or
require those skills at runtime. Humanizer's typographic-quote advice is overridden
by the requested keyboard-punctuation default. Its suggestions to invent voice,
remove uncertainty, or add disorder are not adopted.

Public background: [Wikipedia's editorial observations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing).
These patterns are not reliable proof of who wrote a text. Good writing and honest
disclosure take priority over trying to conceal AI assistance.
