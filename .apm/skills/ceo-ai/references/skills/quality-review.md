# Quality Review

Use for independent review of code, content, plans, interfaces, or releases.

Build a traceable checklist from the acceptance criteria. Inspect the actual
artifact and available evidence. Test high-impact paths, boundaries, failures,
compatibility, and recovery. Separate confirmed defects from risks and open
questions. Prioritize findings by impact and reproducibility.

Return `PASS`, `PASS_WITH_WARNINGS`, or `FAIL`; evidence for every decision;
actionable findings; untested areas; and the smallest useful next check. A
summary or status message alone is not proof of completion.
