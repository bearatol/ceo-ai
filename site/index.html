CEO AI / Q&A

A few things to know.

Open the help guide ↗
What does it install?+

One skill with its supporting instructions. No new model, accounts or automatic registration of other agents.

Will it just agree with me?+

It is instructed to question weak assumptions and explain its objections. You make the decision; it should adapt when new evidence appears.

What has been tested?+

File installation, updates and removal in macOS fixture projects for all three tools, including APM 0.31.0. Linux and macOS CI checks pass. Invocation inside the apps and model behavior remain unverified.

What if installation stops?+

Keep your files. Ask the agent to explain the conflict or missing permission. The help guide covers missing skills, interrupted updates and older installations.

AI can be wrong. A second AI review can be wrong too. CEO gains no extra permissions and does not replace your project’s required security reviews.
