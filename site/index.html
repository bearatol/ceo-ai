Check my skills↗

The first scan needs no Docker.

Python 3.10+ on macOS, Linux or native Windows (fixed local NTFS). Bounded static analysis, with no LLM/API calls. Your AI app’s chat tokens are separate. Windows rejects network drives, reparse points and package aliases.

Prepare and run an additional scan

Copy this request into your AI app. The agent checks compatibility, guides Docker setup and prepares the image. You do not need to choose image IDs or write commands.

Current recipe: native ARM64 Docker host on macOS or Linux. Native Windows builtin indexing does not require Docker.

CEO AI / Your kind of work

Same questions. Different professions.

CEO started with software work. The approach also helps with a brief, a proposal or a research question: clarify the goal, challenge the plan, do the work and check it.

01DevelopmentFix the cause before rewriting+

Your task

“Reports take too long. Should we rewrite the service?”

How CEO helps

Checks measurements, compares a targeted fix with a rewrite, and plans implementation and tests.

What you can produce

A measured change, a test report and a decision about the rewrite.

02MarketingTurn an idea into a test+

Your task

“We have a new product and a limited launch budget.”

How CEO helps

Clarifies the audience and offer, challenges channel assumptions, drafts campaign messages and a small experiment.

What you can produce

A campaign brief, message drafts and a plan for measuring the test.

03SalesPrepare a relevant proposal+

Your task

“Help me prepare for a meeting with a potential customer.”

How CEO helps

Uses your notes to identify the customer’s problem, unanswered questions and unsupported promises in the offer.

What you can produce

A meeting agenda, discovery questions and a proposal draft to review.

04ManagementChoose what the team should do first+

Your task

“Three projects matter, but the team can only take on one.”

How CEO helps

Compares goals, effort, dependencies and the cost of delay. Challenges priorities and asks what would change the decision.

What you can produce

A decision note, a sequence of work and checkpoints for reconsidering it.

05DesignCheck a brief before drawing screens+

Your task

“Users abandon this flow. Help me decide what to change.”

How CEO helps

Reviews the available evidence, separates usability problems from assumptions and compares design options.

What you can produce

A revised brief, user-flow options and a usability test plan.

06ResearchFind what the evidence really supports+

Your task

“These sources disagree. Help me make sense of them.”

How CEO helps

Compares the sources you provide, separates findings from assumptions and flags missing evidence. Web research needs browsing tools.

What you can produce

A comparison with source references, open questions and a next research step.

These are example scenarios, not customer success stories. Results depend on the model, the materials you provide and the tools your app can access. CEO does not automatically gain access to a CRM, ad account or design tool.

CEO AI / Q&A

A few things to know.

CEO AI is under active development. We test and refine the orchestration skill based on test results and feedback.

Open the help guide ↗
What does it install?+

One native plugin with CEO, Writer and Security Scan. The project installer provides only the core skill. No new model, accounts or automatic agent registration.

Do I need Docker?+

No Docker is needed for the builtin check. The optional CEO AI Security module requires it. See the separate Docker walkthrough for the supported platform and setup.

Will it just agree with me?+

CEO checks whether your proposed solution serves your goal. Recommendations should follow evidence, authoritative primary sources and applicable established practices, with gaps stated clearly. You decide; neither agreement nor disagreement is automatic.

What has been tested?+

Native plugin installation, updates, removal and skill discovery were checked on macOS. The project installer is checked in Windows, Linux and macOS CI. Model invocation remains unverified. See the plugin guide for versions and limits.

What if installation stops?+

Keep your files. Ask the agent to explain the conflict or missing permission. The help guide covers missing skills, interrupted updates and older installations.

AI can be wrong. A second AI review can be wrong too. CEO gains no extra permissions and does not replace your project’s required security reviews.
